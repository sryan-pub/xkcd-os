# Support

xkcd: Open Source is a free, open-source reader for [xkcd](https://xkcd.com) on iPhone and iPad.

## Getting help

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/sryan-pub/xkcd-os/issues).
Please include your device, its iOS version and the comic number if it is about a particular
comic.

## Common questions

**A comic looks blurry for a moment.** Small comics are sharpened on your device right after
they load, which takes a fraction of a second.

**New-comic alerts arrive late.** iOS decides when the app may check for new comics in the
background, so an alert can come some hours after a comic is published.

**Interactive comics.** They play inside the app; some need a keyboard or are slow on older
devices.

**Favorites did not sync.** Sync uses iCloud; check that iCloud is on for the app in Settings.
