# Privacy policy

*xkcd: Open Source, last updated 13 November 2026*

The app doesn't collect anything about you. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking,
and no server of its own.

## What stays on your device

Your favorites, the comics you've read and your settings are stored on your device. If you use
iCloud, they sync between your devices through iCloud key-value storage in your own iCloud
account. I can't see them.

## Who the app talks to

- xkcd.com and imgs.xkcd.com, to fetch comics and their images and to show interactive comics
- explainxkcd.com, only when you ask for a comic's explanation
- Apple, for iCloud sync and the App Store

These sites receive the ordinary information any web request carries, such as your IP address.
Their own privacy policies apply to that.

## Notifications and widgets

New-comic alerts are optional. Your device checks xkcd.com for a new comic and creates the
alert locally. Widgets read the same data the app keeps on your device.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions:
